# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Wine enthusiasts managing a personal, self-hosted cellar from desktop and mobile.
They need to find a bottle quickly, understand stock at a glance, add or scan wine,
and consult tasting and origin details without administrative friction.

## Product Purpose

Wine Cellar turns inventory management into a personal tasting notebook. Success means
the collection feels inviting to browse while stock, location, vintage, value, and
drink-by information remain immediately actionable.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, warm, knowledgeable. The voice evokes a carefully kept cellar and an annotated
tasting journal without becoming precious or ceremonial.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic Bootstrap admin, a cold enterprise dashboard, a neon dark-mode
SaaS, or a decorative wine shop that hides operational information.

## Design Principles

- Make the collection the protagonist, not the software.
- Combine editorial warmth with familiar, efficient product controls.
- Reveal richness progressively; keep primary actions obvious.
- Preserve confidence through consistent components and plain language.
- Design mobile as a first-class cellar companion, not a compressed desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast,
44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, color-independent status cues, and responsive
operation from 320px upward.
